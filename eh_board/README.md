# Dashboard Builder

Build Odoo dashboards from defined business metrics, your accessible Odoo data, uploaded files or administrator-configured remote snapshots. Preview a business pack before creating a private draft. Each business widget records its definition version and company.

Version 7 adds Sales, Cash and Receivables, and Warehouse packs; optional AI authoring from permitted metrics; consistent analysis context for insights and exports; and scheduled Google Sheets/HTTPS JSON sources. Existing Smart Build, chart editor, pivot, map, filters, drill, layout history, alerts and exports remain available.

Use the release package matching your Odoo major version. Read [release notes and setup](doc/RELEASE_7.md) before upgrading or configuring AI and remote sources. Python dependencies, provider charges, access rights and business limitations are documented there.

The core is free under OPL-1. AI usage is billed by your chosen provider. Response time depends on the database, workload and deployment; no fixed performance claim is made.
